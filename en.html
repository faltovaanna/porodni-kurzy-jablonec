Group prenatal class

Complete preparation.

An intensive class for you and your companion — ideally from week 30 of pregnancy. Together we go through everything from the last weeks of waiting to the first days with your baby.

01

The end of pregnancy

Common discomforts and how to ease them · when to head to the hospital · what to pack · a birth plan that makes sense.

02

Birth step by step

Stages of labour and what to expect · natural management of birth · pain relief options · comfort positions and breathing · the partner's role during birth.

03

Your baby is here — the postpartum weeks

The first moments with your baby (bonding) · what happens after birth · taking care of yourself postpartum · what to prepare for at home.

Hands-on practice & your questions

You will try positions, work with the ball and practise breathing. And above all — ask anything. From midwives, not from the internet.

Frequently asked questions

What you ask us

When is the best time to take the class?
Ideally around week 30 of pregnancy — early enough to absorb and practise everything, and late enough for the information to still be fresh. But you are welcome at any time; we will adapt the class.
Do I have to come with my partner?
No — you can do the class on your own. We do recommend bringing a companion though: they will learn how to really support you during birth, and you will both feel more confident.
I want to give birth in a different hospital. Does the class still make sense?
Absolutely! The class covers information you can use in any maternity hospital.
Will my health insurance cover the class?
In most cases, yes — if you are insured in the Czech public health insurance system. For 2026, VZP and ZP MV ČR contribute up to 5,000 CZK, ČPZP and OZP up to 3,000 CZK, VoZP up to 2,000 CZK (RBP 1,000 CZK, ZPŠ 500 CZK). The usual condition is that the class is led by a midwife — which we meet, as we are registered healthcare providers. After the class we issue an invoice and, once paid, a payment confirmation; you send these documents to your insurer. If anything is unclear, just ask us.
How do I sign up and how does payment work?
You can sign up by email, and for group classes also through the booking system of Jiná Huť. We confirm your spot right away; payment is by bank transfer in advance. If you cannot attend, we will offer you a replacement date.
What should I bring?
Just comfortable clothes and a good mood. Everything else — materials, mats, balls and refreshments — is ready for you.
Where does the class take place and where can I park?
In the lovely space Jiná Huť at Lovecká 561/14, Jablonec nad Nisou — a 10-minute walk from the centre. Free parking is available on the street and nearby.
Do you offer a private class just for us?
Yes — you can have the whole class just for yourself and your companion, at a time that suits you, at your home. Write to us and we will arrange the details.
